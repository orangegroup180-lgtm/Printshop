Who We Are

Built on Quality, Driven by Purpose

Cosmicol Ltd is a dynamic and forward-thinking company established in 2017 and fully registered under the laws of Zambia. We specialize in design, printing, corporate branding, and general supply services, delivering tailored solutions to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

Our strength lies in our commitment to quality, efficiency, and innovation. We take pride in providing reliable products and services that help businesses enhance their brand presence and operational effectiveness.

At Cosmicol Ltd, we go beyond supply — we build lasting partnerships. By understanding our clients' goals, we provide solutions that improve productivity, support growth, and maximize value. Our focus is to ensure every project is handled with professionalism, precision, and a dedication to excellence.

Our Journey

Growing From the Ground Up

2017

Founded in Ndola

Cosmicol Limited was established and fully registered under the laws of Zambia, beginning operations in design and printing.

2018

Expanded into Vehicle Branding

Added full vehicle wrapping and fleet branding services, quickly becoming a go-to partner for Ndola businesses.

2020

Billboard Advertising Launched

Secured premium billboard placements near Mulungushi Bus Station, offering high-visibility outdoor advertising in Ndola.

2022

Corporate Gifts Division

Launched a full corporate gifts catalogue — branded flasks, keychains, bags, and promotional merchandise for businesses of all sizes.

Now

Serving 200+ Clients Across the Copperbelt

Today, Cosmicol serves a diverse client base — from SMEs and NGOs to large corporates — delivering quality on every project.
